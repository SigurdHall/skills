# Meeting Prep Narrative

Use this for agendas, executive meetings, decision meetings, stakeholder
alignment, workshops, pre-reads, briefing notes, and Q&A prep.

## Sources And Influences

- Amazon-style narrative memo practice: use writing to force clarity before a
  meeting; let the document carry complete reasoning when the stakes are high.
- Decision-meeting discipline: every agenda item should have an intended
  outcome.

## Meeting Prep Workflow

1. Define the meeting type: inform, align, decide, unblock, negotiate, teach,
   or get mandate.
2. Write the desired meeting outcome in one sentence.
3. Identify decisions that must be made in the meeting versus later.
4. Decide whether the meeting needs a slide deck, memo, pre-read, one-pager, or
   no artifact.
5. Map participants:
   - decision owner
   - sponsor
   - blocker
   - expert
   - implementer
   - affected party
6. Prepare agenda by outcome, not topic.
7. Prepare objections and answers.
8. Prepare the follow-up note before the meeting: decision, owner, deadline,
   open questions.

## Agenda Pattern

| Time | Segment | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 | Frame | Confirm purpose and decision needed |
| 5-15 | Context | Establish shared facts |
| 15-30 | Options | Clarify choices and trade-offs |
| 30-45 | Recommendation | Discuss preferred path |
| 45-55 | Decision | Confirm mandate, scope, owner, next step |
| 55-60 | Close | Capture actions and unresolved issues |

## Pre-Read Pattern

1. Decision needed.
2. Recommendation.
3. Context.
4. Options considered.
5. Evidence.
6. Risks and mitigations.
7. Ask.
8. Appendix.

## Checks

- Is there a real decision or only a discussion?
- Who can say yes?
- What does a "yes" authorize?
- What would a "no" mean?
- What can be deferred?
- What will be written in the meeting notes?
